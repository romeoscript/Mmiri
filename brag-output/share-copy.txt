Smallholder farmers can't buy drought cover, because assessing a two-hectare farm costs
more than the policy is worth.

Mmiri removes the assessor. Five underwriter agents bid sealed premiums for the risk, the
lowest wins and locks the full payout as collateral, and NASA's rainfall record settles it.
Nobody files a claim.

Backtested on 15 seasons of Nigerian rainfall: the agent that priced on all 45 years won
80% of the market and lost most of its capital. The one that trusted recent seasons made
money. Nobody told these agents the climate is drying — the rainfall did.

Built at NEAR HACKATHON. @NEARProtocol #NEARHackathon

Repo: https://github.com/romeoscript/Mmiri

it. No claim form.
